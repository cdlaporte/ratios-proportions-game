RATIO ROGUE

Fight monsters. Grab loot. The locks only open with math.

How to Play

WASDMove ←↑↓→ or clickShoot (hold to keep firing) SpaceDash (you can't be hurt while dashing) EOpen chests, use shrines, buy things, go down hatches P / EscPause
  • Clear rooms of monsters to open the doors. Find the boss to reach the next floor. Beat 3 floors to win!
  • 🔒 Bow-Tie Chests are locked with proportions. Cross multiply to open them and choose a power-up.
  • 📐 Angle Shrines heal you if you solve an angle ratio problem.
  • 🌉 Bridges are built by solving triangle side + perimeter problems.
  • 🔮 Boss shields break when you solve the glowing rune.
  • ⭐ Perfect solve = first try with no hints, for a bigger reward. Wrong answers never hurt you, so keep trying! After 2 misses you can see the full solution.
  • Keep scratch paper handy. Every answer is a whole number.
